========================================
时间: 2026-09-16 (真云补测)
工具: huaweicloud_auth_switch (mode=memory action=persist + securityToken)

实测调用: auth_switch {mode:memory, action:persist, ak:<S1同ak>, sk:FAKE, securityToken:FAKETOKEN}

断言2: S1(credentials.json).securityToken 保持空串(未落盘) -> PASS
